Scene 5: The Confrontation
EXT. ROOFTOP - NIGHT
The wind howls violently, whipping loose gravel across the tar-paper roof. Beatrice stands dangerously close to the edge of the building, dangling the silver watch by its delicate chain over the sheer, ten-story drop. Arthur emerges from the rooftop stairwell, breathless, shielding his eyes from the blinding, scorching midday sun beating down on them.
ARTHUR
Don't do it, Beatrice. That watch is the only evidence we have left to clear Charles's name.
BEATRICE
Evidence of what, Arthur? That time is always slipping away? That no matter how hard you hold onto the past, it just pulls you down with it?
She opens her hand, releasing the chain. Arthur rushes to the ledge, watching the silver glint catch the light as it disappears into the dark, shadowy abyss of the city below, leaving only the sound of distant police sirens echoing up through the concrete canyon.
