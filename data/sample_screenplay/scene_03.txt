Scene 3: The Panic
INT. CHARLES’S APARTMENT - NIGHT
Dim, dusty, and smelling faintly of stale bread. Arthur kicks the flimsy wooden door open, his gun drawn. He finds Charles frantically packing a leather suitcase, tossing wrinkled shirts and old photographs into it with reckless abandon. The silver pocket watch is clutched tightly in Charles's left hand.
ARTHUR
Going somewhere? I told you to stay put and keep the blinds drawn.
CHARLES
She’s coming for it! You don't understand who you're dealing with, Arthur! You have to help me get out of the city!
Charles panics as a loud thud echoes from the hallway. He drops the silver watch onto the rug, sprints across the room, and effortlessly lifts a massive, solid-oak steamer trunk with both of his bare hands, sliding it across the floor to barricade the front door.
ARTHUR
Calm down! We have the upper hand if we just think this through.
