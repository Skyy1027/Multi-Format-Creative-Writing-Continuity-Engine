Scene 2: The Ambush
EXT. ALLEYWAY - LATER
The rain has stopped, leaving the cobblestones slick and reflecting the amber glow of the streetlamps. Arthur walks briskly down the narrow corridor, glancing over his shoulder every few steps. The shadows seem to stretch too far.
Suddenly, Beatrice steps out from behind a rusted dumpster, blocking his path entirely.
BEATRICE
Hand over the timepiece, Arthur. It doesn't belong to the fisherman, and it certainly doesn't belong to a two-bit sleuth like you.
Arthur stops, his jaw tight. He tightens his grip on his bright yellow umbrella, pointing the sharp metallic tip at her like a fencing foil.
ARTHUR
Finders keepers, Beatrice. You know how this city works. The man hired me to protect his property.
BEATRICE
Property is a very loose term down here.
Beatrice smirks, kicks a rolling metal trash can violently toward his shins, and vanishes into the thick, rolling fog before he can even react to the clang of the metal.
