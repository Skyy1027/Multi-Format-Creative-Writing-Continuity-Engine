Scene 4: The Escape
INT. CHARLES’S APARTMENT - CONTINUOUS
Beatrice descends rapidly from the rusted iron fire escape, stepping gracefully onto the wet asphalt. The neon streetlights reflect perfectly off the deep, oily puddles surrounding her leather boots as a passing yellow taxi honks loudly, splashing water onto her trench coat. She bends down and picks up the silver watch from the damp, cracked sidewalk, inspecting its intricate engravings under the glow of a streetlamp.
