Scene 1: The Setup
INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY
Rain batters the large glass window, blurring the neon signs of the city outside. ARTHUR, a weary private eye holding a dripping red umbrella, sits across from CHARLES, a nervous, twitchy man whose right arm is heavily wrapped in a thick, immobilizing plaster cast. A tarnished silver pocket watch rests on the sticky Formica table between them.
ARTHUR
You expect me to believe you found this in the river? A two-hundred-year-old heirloom, just washing up on the muddy banks?
CHARLES
I swear it! I was fishing by the south docks. The watch was just sitting inside the belly of a dead catfish. It didn't even have water damage, Arthur. You have to believe me, people are looking for it!
Arthur sighs, tapping his fingers against his coffee mug.
ARTHUR
People are always looking for something, Charles. That doesn't mean you need to drag me into it.
In the corner booth, BEATRICE, wearing a dark trench coat and a wide-brimmed hat, subtly lowers her newspaper. She eyes the silver pocket watch with intense focus, gently stirring a cup of hot black tea with a wooden spoon. She takes a slow sip, never breaking her gaze from their table.
