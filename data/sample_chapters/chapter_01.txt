The road from Ashenmoor wound through a forest of black-barked oaks that seemed to drink the light from the sky. Kael walked at the head of the group, his boots crunching on the frost-hardened ground. The sellsword kept one hand resting on the pommel of Dawnfang, the enchanted longsword he had won from a dying knight in the siege of Thornwall. Its blade, so they said, could cut through shadow itself. Slung across his back was a leather pack containing a coil of hempen rope, a tinderbox, and three days' worth of dried venison.

Behind him came Liora, her green cloak pulled tight against the cold. The alchemist carried a satchel full of her trade: two vials of healing salve, a pouch of flash powder, and a leather-bound journal in which she recorded every formula she had ever devised. She walked with a slight limp, a reminder of the fever that had nearly killed her as a child, but her dark eyes were sharp and missed nothing.

They had set out from the village of Ashenmoor at first light, hired by the village elder to investigate a series of disappearances in the Greymist Marshes to the north. Three shepherds had vanished in as many weeks, and the villagers whispered of a creature in the fog.

"We should reach the marsh by nightfall," Kael said without turning around. He consulted a hand-drawn map the elder had given him and folded it back into his belt pouch.

Liora caught up to walk beside him. "The elder mentioned a waystation — an old hunter's lodge at the edge of the marsh. If it still stands, we can use it as a base."

Kael nodded. "Better than sleeping in the open with whatever took those shepherds prowling about."

By late afternoon they reached the tree line. The forest ended abruptly, giving way to a vast expanse of grey-green wetland that stretched to the horizon. Mist hung in low sheets over the water, and the air smelled of peat and decay. True to the elder's word, a squat stone lodge sat at the edge of the marsh, its roof half-collapsed but its walls still solid.

They made camp inside. Kael used his tinderbox to start a fire in the old hearth while Liora examined the walls, finding old tally marks scratched into the stone — someone else's count of days spent waiting.

"We start searching at dawn," Kael said, sharpening Dawnfang by the firelight. "Stay together. Whatever is out there is bold enough to take grown men."

Liora nodded, uncorking one of her healing salves to treat a blister on her heel. She made a note in her journal about the marsh's geography before settling in for the night.

They were at the waystation lodge, at the edge of the Greymist Marshes. The hunt would begin tomorrow.
